# Kronwerke

Kronwerke is a Minecraft server for streamers and their viewers. A streamer joins the project and gets a handful of whitelist slots to bring people from their own chat. Everyone plays on one world, in one modpack, for one season of about two months. The season has an arc: the server starts small and unlocks more as the community reaches goals together.

## How a season works

The pack is split into stages. In the first stage, the tools are simple: a bit of Create, the first spells of Ars Nouveau, the basics. Deeper machines and darker magic are locked, for everyone, no matter how much modded experience someone brings.

Each stage ends with a community goal. It is deliberately too big for one person: ten thousand cobblestone at the spawn, a few thousand ingots, later stranger things. Anyone can pay in, the boss bar shows how far the server is, and when the goal is reached the next stage opens for the whole server at once. Streams turn that moment into an event.

Inside a stage, quests take every player from "what is this mod" to "I built that". Someone who has never seen a modded pack has a path. Someone who has played every kitchen sink pack has a stage full of things to optimise, and a community goal to push.

## Season 2

Winter 2026/27, NeoForge 1.21.1. Technology and magic in equal parts: Create, Mekanism, Applied Energistics on one side, Ars Nouveau, Botania, Occultism, Theurgy and friends on the other, with recipes that tie the two together instead of letting them run side by side. A boss at the end that is not the Ender Dragon.

Season 1 ran in spring 2026 with about ten regulars. It taught us two things: run the server on mods, not on plugins bolted to a hybrid server, and do not let the experienced players reach the endgame while the newcomers are still looking for iron. Both are what Season 2 is built around.

## Repositories

| Repository | What |
| --- | --- |
| [pack](https://github.com/kronwerke/pack) | The modpack, managed with packwiz, exported for Modrinth and CurseForge |
| [core](https://github.com/kronwerke/core) | The server mod: whitelist slots per streamer, community goals, staged progression |
| [botania](https://github.com/kronwerke/botania) | Botania built from the upstream 1.21.1 porting branch, until there is an official release |

Bugs and ideas go into the issues of the repository they belong to. Everything else happens on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/DZs3XyyHDj).
